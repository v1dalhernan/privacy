Our Core Privacy Architecture

Three foundational commitments that apply across our entire software portfolio.

1. Sovereign Data

We believe your personal schedules and database diagrams belong solely to you. Everything is written to your device’s sandbox and sandboxed storage.

2. No Silent Cloud Telemetry

We do not bundle surveillance SDKs or ad networks. When cloud services are used (such as Google Gemini AI parsing), they are activated strictly on your demand.

3. Direct Accountability

No bureaucratic walls. If you have questions or concerns about how our apps handle files or permissions, email us directly and receive human responses.
