School Schedule app

Support • Last updated November 16, 2026
Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Write to us at support@threedors.com. You can write in any of the app’s languages; we usually answer within a few days. You can also use Settings → Feedback inside the app.

Sync between devices

  • iPhone and iPad: go to Settings → Backup & sync and tap Turn on iCloud. Do the same on your other device and tap Sync now. You need to be signed in to iCloud on the device.
  • Android: in the same screen, tap Connect Google Drive.
  • Task attachments (photos, files and audio) are not synced. To take them too, use a backup file or Move to another phone.

Move to a new phone (also between iPhone and Android)

In Settings → Backup & sync → Move to another phone, tap Send from this phone and send yourself the file (messaging, email, AirDrop…). On the new phone, install the app, open the same screen and tap Receive on this phone to open the file. Everything moves: schedules, subjects, tasks with their attachments, notes, exams and settings.

Reminders don’t arrive

  • Check that notifications are allowed for School Schedule in your device settings, and turned on in Settings → Notifications inside the app.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the system keeps a limited number of scheduled reminders. Opening the app now and then refreshes them; if you see “Keep your reminders coming”, just open the app.
  • Keep Background App Refresh on for the app (iPhone) and avoid battery restrictions for it (Android), so reminders can be renewed in the background.

Backups and restoring

Settings → Backup & sync → Export backup creates a file you can keep anywhere. Restore backup opens it again and replaces the current data; if you change your mind, Undo last restore brings back what you had before.

Medals and purchases

Medals are optional one-time donations that support the app; every feature is free. If you reinstall the app or change device, open Settings → Support the app and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID or Google account. Refunds are handled by the store: Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play.

Delete your data

All data lives on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it. If you used cloud sync, tap Delete cloud data in Backup & sync to remove the copy from your iCloud or Google Drive.

Privacy

The app has no ads, no accounts and no tracking. Read the full Privacy Policy.
