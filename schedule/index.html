Privacy Policy

Application: School Schedule app • Publisher: JhovTech / Threedors • Last updated: June 12, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how the application School Schedule (the “App”), published by JhovTech, collects, uses, and protects user information. By using this application, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

By default, the app does not collect any personal or sensitive user data. All schedule scanning uses on-device processing — no image or text leaves your device.

  • Optional AI scanning: If you explicitly choose the "With AI" option in the schedule scan feature, the image of your schedule is sent to Google's Gemini API for processing. This is strictly opt-in — the default "On device" option never transmits data. When using the AI option, Google's privacy policy applies to that request.
  • For AI-assisted scanning, data is processed by Google's Gemini API. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Additionally, Google Play services may provide anonymous technical information such as crash reports.

2. How We Use Information

We do not use any personal data for commercial, marketing, or tracking purposes. Any information received is used solely to improve the functionality and stability of the application.

3. Information Sharing

We do not share personal or sensitive user information with any third parties. If in the future data collection or sharing becomes necessary, we will request the user’s explicit consent in compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

4. Data Security

We apply reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect any data from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. While we do not store personal user data, we follow industry best practices to ensure app security.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not store personal data. Any anonymous technical information received through automated services is retained only for the minimum necessary time and then securely deleted.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Users will be notified of any changes through an update to this page.
