School Schedule app

Privacy Policy • Last updated November 16, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how the application School Schedule (the “App”), published by JhovTech and distributed through Threedors, collects, uses, and protects user information. By using this application, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

By default, the app does not collect any personal or sensitive user data. All schedule scanning and timetable entries use on-device processing — no image or text leaves your device during standard use.

  • Optional cloud sync: Sync is off until you turn it on in “Backup & sync”. A copy of your app data (schedules, subjects, classes, tasks, grades, attendance, exams and app settings) is stored in your own cloud account so it can be synced between your devices. Task attachments are not synced. The data never passes through servers of ours, and we have no access to it.
    • iPhone and iPad — iCloud: the copy is stored in the app’s private space in your own iCloud account (CloudKit private database), together with a revision identifier, the data format version and the name of the operating system (“ios” or “android”) that last synced. No sign-in is needed beyond your device’s iCloud account. Apple’s Privacy Policy applies to data stored in iCloud.
    • Android — Google Drive: the app signs in with Google and requests access only to a private app folder in your own Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). The app cannot see any other file in your Drive. The email address of the connected account is stored only on your device to show which account is in use. Google’s Privacy Policy applies to data stored in Google Drive.
  • Schedule scanning: Scanning a schedule from the camera, a photo or a PDF is processed on your device. With “Use my AI”, the app opens your device’s share sheet so you can send the image to an AI app of your choice; it goes only to the app you pick, under that app’s own privacy policy, and we never receive it.
  • Optional AI scanning (some earlier Android versions only): If you explicitly chose the “With AI” scan option, the image of your schedule was sent to Google’s Gemini API to read the timetable. It was strictly opt-in, and Google’s Privacy Policy applied to that request. Current versions of the app do not include this option.
  • Reminders: Class, task and exam reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No data is sent anywhere to deliver them.
  • Backup files: You can export your data to a backup file. The file is created on your device and only leaves it if you choose to share or save it somewhere (for example by email, messaging, AirDrop or cloud storage).
  • Optional donations: Supporting the app with a medal is processed entirely by Google Play Billing (Android) or the Apple App Store (iPhone and iPad). We do not receive your payment details; the app only receives confirmation of which medal was purchased.
  • Rating prompt: The app may ask you to rate it using Google Play’s in-app review or Apple’s App Store rating prompt. To decide when to ask, it keeps simple usage counters (such as days of use) on your device only; they are never sent anywhere.
  • Crash diagnostics: Google Play services (Android) may provide anonymous technical diagnostic information, such as system version and crash reports. On iPhone and iPad, Apple shares anonymous crash reports with developers only if you allow it in your device settings (“Share With App Developers”). We use them only to fix issues and improve stability.

2. How We Use Information

We do not use any personal data for commercial, marketing, or tracking purposes. Any technical diagnostics received through automated services are used solely to improve the functionality and stability of the application.

3. Information Sharing

We do not share personal or sensitive user information with any third parties. Data is only sent to Google or Apple when you explicitly use one of their services described above (cloud sync, purchases or, in earlier Android versions, AI scanning), and only to perform that feature. If in the future data collection or sharing becomes necessary, we will request the user’s explicit consent in compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

4. Data Security

We apply reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect any data from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. While we do not store personal user data on external servers, we follow industry best practices to ensure on-device security. Cloud sync uses the encrypted connections of iCloud or Google Drive and your own Apple or Google account.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not store personal data on remote servers. All your timetable data resides locally within your device’s private app storage. Uninstalling the application or clearing its storage through your device settings deletes all local data immediately.

If you enabled cloud sync, the synced copy stays in your iCloud or Google Drive until you delete it. You can remove it at any time with “Delete cloud data” in the app’s “Backup & sync” screen. On iPhone and iPad you can also remove it from your iCloud storage settings. On Android, you can also delete it from Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps → Schedule → Delete hidden app data), and disconnecting the account stops syncing and revokes the app’s access.

6. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Users will be notified of any changes through an update to this page.
