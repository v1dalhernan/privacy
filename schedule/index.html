This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which School Schedule (the “App”), published by JhovTech and distributed through Threedors, handles and protects information. By installing or utilizing the application, you agree to the data boundaries articulated herein.

1. Information We Collect

By default, the application does not collect, record, or transmit personal or sensitive user data. All schedule scanning and timetable creation relies entirely on on-device processing: no image or timetable text ever leaves your device during standard operation.

  • Optional AI schedule scanning: If you explicitly choose the “With AI” option in the schedule scan feature, the image of your timetable is securely transmitted to Google’s Gemini API exclusively to perform optical recognition and structure extraction. This feature is strictly opt-in; the default “On device” option operates entirely locally and transmits no data. When utilizing the AI option, Google’s Privacy Policy governs that processing request.
  • Crash and diagnostic telemetry: Google Play Services may aggregate anonymous technical information, such as system version and crash traces, solely to diagnose malfunctions and maintain application reliability.

2. How We Use Information

We do not process, monetize, or utilize any personal data for commercial, advertising, behavioral profiling, or tracking purposes. Any telemetry received through platform channels is utilized strictly to improve application stability and performance.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share personal or sensitive user information with any third-party brokers or external organizations. If in the future additional data exchange becomes technically necessary to deliver user-requested integrations, explicit and granular user consent will be obtained prior to any transmission in strict compliance with applicable regulatory frameworks.

4. Data Security Standards

We implement administrative and technical safeguards designed to safeguard app integrity and prevent unauthorized manipulation. Because personal timetables and preferences are contained within your device’s isolated sandbox, data protection adheres to native operating system sandboxing principles.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We maintain no remote repositories or user accounts storing personal data. You retain unconditional ownership over your information: deleting the application or clearing application storage through your operating system settings permanently expunges all stored timetables and configurations immediately.

6. Device Permissions

The application may request the following platform permissions:

  • Camera / Photo Gallery: Required exclusively when you choose to capture or import an image of a physical schedule for on-device parsing.
  • Notifications & Exact Alarms: Used solely to alert you to upcoming classes, periods, and scheduled events at the precise times you designate.

7. Revisions to this Policy

We reserve the right to revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements in app functionality or statutory requirements. Continued use of the application following published revisions constitutes acceptance of the modified terms.
