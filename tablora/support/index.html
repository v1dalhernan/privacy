Tablora: Database Diagrams

Support • Last updated November 16, 2026
Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Write to us at support@threedors.com. You can write in any of the app’s languages; we usually answer within a few days. You can also use Settings → Send feedback inside the app.

Import an existing schema

Tap Import SQL / DBML and paste your SQL script or DBML. Tablora creates a new diagram with its tables and relations; your other diagrams don’t change. If the import fails, check the code and try again — the error tells you what couldn’t be read.

Export your design

  • Export diagram gives you SQL DDL, DBML or a PNG image (with a transparent, light, dark or current-theme background), ready to copy, share or save.
  • On iPhone and iPad, saving the image to Photos asks for permission to add photos. If you denied it, allow it again in Settings → Tablora → Photos.

Where are my diagrams saved?

On your device only: changes are saved automatically and there is no account or cloud sync. To keep a portable, editable copy or move a diagram to another device, use Save as… to create a .dbd document, then open it on the other device with Import .dbd file.

Medals and purchases

Medals are optional one-time donations that support the app; every feature is free. If you reinstall the app or change device, open Support the app in Settings and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID or Google account. Refunds are handled by the store: Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play.

Language

Tablora is available in eight languages. Change it in Settings → Language, or let it follow your system’s language.

Delete your data

Delete a diagram from the app at any time (this can’t be undone). Uninstalling Tablora deletes every diagram stored in it; files you exported stay wherever you saved them.

Privacy

Tablora has no ads, no accounts and no tracking, and your diagrams never leave your device unless you export them. Read the full Privacy Policy.
