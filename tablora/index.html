Tablora is a visual database schema modeling tool published by Threedors / Jhonathan Vidal. It is designed to work entirely locally on your device without requiring user accounts or external network infrastructure.

1. Projects, Schemas, and Storage

Tablora enables software developers, students, and architects to design relational database schemas. All projects, tables, relationships, notes, and visual canvas coordinates are stored strictly inside the private sandbox storage of the application on your physical device.

  • No Account Required: You do not register, log in, or authenticate with any proprietary Tablora cloud service.
  • No Live Database Queries: Tablora is an offline modeling workspace. It does not establish network connections to PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite, or any remote database servers.
  • No Analytics or Ads: The production application contains no tracking pixels, third-party analytics SDKs, or targeted advertising frameworks.

2. Optional In-App Purchases & Developer Support

Tablora offers optional one-time medal purchases to support ongoing development:

  • Billing Channel: All financial transactions, purchases, and license restorations are processed exclusively by Google Play in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Financial Information: Tablora never requests, receives, or stores credit card numbers, billing addresses, or financial credentials. Google Play handles all payment execution and solely returns confirmation tokens to activate medal status locally.
  • While the core schema editor functions offline, purchasing or restoring medals requires access to Google Play Services.

3. File Import, Export, and System Permissions

When you work with diagram files, permissions are handled transparently:

  • Importing Schemas: When selecting a SQL or DBML file, Tablora parses the structure locally in memory to generate visual canvas components. No file content is sent to external servers.
  • Exporting Diagrams & Code: You can export diagrams as PNG images or generate SQL scripts. File delivery utilizes your operating system’s native file picker or system share sheet, ensuring you retain total control over the file destination.
  • Storage Permissions: On modern Android and iOS platforms, Tablora utilizes system-managed picker APIs that require no broad storage permissions. On legacy platforms (Android 10 and below), media write access is scoped strictly to saving exported diagram images. Tablora never accesses or inspects your personal photo library.

4. Data Retention, Backup, and Deletion

Because all diagram data is stored locally:

  • You can delete any project or diagram directly within the application at any time.
  • Clearing the app’s cache/data or uninstalling the app immediately expunges all stored local schema files.
  • Medal purchases remain associated with your Google Play account and can be restored whenever you reinstall the app. Transaction histories and refund requests are administered through your Google Play account settings.

5. Security & Operating System Sandboxing

We employ secure software engineering practices and leverage native operating system application sandboxes to isolate your project files from other applications installed on your device.

6. Policy Updates & Contact Information

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features, schema formats, or statutory requirements. Changes will be posted to this URL with an updated revision date.
