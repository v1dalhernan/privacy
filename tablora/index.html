Tablora: Database Diagrams

Privacy Policy • Last updated September 15, 2026
Tablora is a visual database modeling tool published by Threedors / Jhonathan Vidal. It is designed to work locally on your device without user accounts, analytics tracking, or proprietary server synchronization.

1. Projects and Preferences

Tablora allows you to design database schemas. On Android, iOS, and macOS, your projects, tables, relationships, and preferences are saved exclusively within the local application storage of your device.

  • Tablora does not require an account or user registration.
  • The application does not include proprietary sync servers, analytics frameworks, or advertising networks.
  • Local storage is managed by the operating system. System backups or restores depend on Android/iOS settings and the user’s personal device backup configuration.

2. Optional In-App Purchases

Tablora includes optional one-time medal purchases to support ongoing development.

  • Google Play processes all purchases and restorations in accordance with its own terms and Privacy Policy.
  • The app receives only the purchase confirmation and retains acquired medal identifiers locally. It never requests, accesses, or stores credit card numbers or financial information.
  • The diagram editor functions entirely offline; purchasing and restoring transactions require connectivity to Google Play Services.

3. Files and Permissions

When you import or export diagrams:

  • Importing: When importing a SQL or DBML file, Tablora parses the selected file locally on your device to generate the visual schema canvas. No file data is transmitted externally.
  • Exporting: You can save diagrams as PNG images or export code, sharing them using your device’s standard system share sheet. The chosen destination app handles the file according to its own policies.
  • Permissions: On modern operating systems, Tablora uses native system pickers that do not require broad storage permissions. On legacy Android versions (API 29 or lower), saving an image may require scoped external storage write permission. Tablora never reads or accesses your photo library.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete diagrams directly from within the app, or remove all local application data through your operating system settings. Exported files remain in the folder or destination you selected until you delete them there.

Uninstalling Tablora deletes all local diagrams stored within the app. Medal purchases can be restored through Google Play upon reinstallation. For payment histories or refunds, use your Google Play account options.

5. Changes to This Policy

We will update this policy and its revision date whenever data handling practices change.
